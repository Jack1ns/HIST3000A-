**Cemetery Network Exercise**
This week I focused on just looking at the cemetery network exercise using the network navigator you gave us and showed us how to use in class. 

With the data given, I made a CSV file looking at the source and target columns between people and how they are related, and I put it into the network navigator and looked at the correlations. 

I looked at the Force Layout, Arc Diagram, and Adjacency Matrix. I think the force layout is the easiest to understand and make sense of; it displays the data in a really nice way. I like how it kept each group separate, rather than how the arc diagram just bunches it all together.

**Things I found**
From the data, the network I made had 29 nodes, which would also mean the data had 29 people and 36 edges, which would mean that's how many connections between people there are. Another cool thing was  that there were 4 different groupings even though there are 5 families because of the relationship between the Hayes Family and the Thompson Family. No other family had a connection like that.
