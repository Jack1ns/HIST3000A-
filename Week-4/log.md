This week, we were at the Billings Bridge Estate, doing what half the class did during week 3 

Gravestone Recording: 
- Used the KoboToolbox form on the iPad to put in information and record gravestones
- Used the Graveyard recording sheet to look at and record different types of tombstones in extreme detail - sometimes too much detail i think
- I had issues with some tombstones not fully fitting the categories, which made recording the tombstones a lengthy process, especially if it did not fit into any categories.
- I thought recording tombstones would be a lot easier and have a lot fewer details than what the coding sheet contained
- The recording software used on the iPad was also interesting in how we can't send it to ourselves since it was designed for areas of the world where having data like that should not be saved onto the device. I think that is a great thing, especially for medical aid and archaeology that might be tied to politics, but for our use, having data that we can send to ourselves is much more useful.

Lidar Scanner:
- this was my first time using a lidar scanner, which i have always wanted to use
- the scanner needed steady movement to capture or it would lose track of where it was
- I never looked at the finale scan we took so im excited to hopefully get a look at that 
- 

Laser Scanner:
- used the laser scanner on a fragment of a tombstone
- it worked really well; I got a good scan from it
- Some issues would be having to remember to keep a constant good distance from the object, as well as being careful about the light you scan in
- The day we had our lab had pretty good lighting, being overcast when I scanned the object, but we noticed another person's scan turned out worse because the sun was on the item at the time.
- The scanner also only picked up the texture of the grass from under the table and did not pick up any texture from the object or even the table it was on, which was interesting.

Photogrammetry: 
- This was really interesting and turned out very well!
- I scanned the objects on one of the tables instead of the large repaired tombstones
- I scanned them as objects and did two different scans: one of a pottery and one of the gravestone fragment, and both turned out very well
- One of the issues I found with this is that it was hard to get detail on surfaces closer to the table where shadows were being cast
