KoboToolBox 

KoboToolbox appears to be a very user-friendly software. It was very easy to set up and start using. 
To test it out, I started by making a new project from scratch. 
Once I figured out how it worked, I thought it would be a good idea to make a quick rough draft of it for my PDAP 
For my PDAP, I am planning on doing something with groundhog holes/burrows around Carleton's campus 
I can map where groundhog holes are to investigate if their locations are associated with characteristics of the surrounding area they are in.
By taking data on this, I can try to find out if there are specific characteristics related to groundhog holes and their location. 

I needed to think of useful questions to have on my KoboToolbox so that i can have a form that will allow me to collect the most useful data to do with groundhog holes
I first started with 
- location of the hole, as that will obviously be one of the most important things
- I then added date discovered to help with data organization 
- I also included the option to name the hole 
- A picture of the hole to help with later data observations
- Closest objects like trees, sidewalks and other things like buildings and walls
- Distance to objects because there might be some kind of correlation to how close holes would be to picnic tables
- Hole size; I'm curious how that varies
- If the hole is active or not, or unknown - I'm not sure how easy this will be to find out, but it will be interesting to know
- I added signs of use to see if there are specific signs that are common, but I added it as a note, thinking that meant I added a note during the filling out of the form, but now I know it's to have a note appear on the form
- if there is vegetation around the hole
- and a field for surrounding environment to see how common things like grass, dirt, trees, shrubs, sloped terrain and others are
- I am also curious how common clusters of holes are and whether they are all connected to the same burrow or not, so I added a yes or no question for that
- and the last one is a numeric value for if there are multiple holes
